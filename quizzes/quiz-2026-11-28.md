## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14.2. How far back in time are we seeing? ✨**

A) ~300 million years after Big Bang
B) ~1 billion years after Big Bang
C) ~500 million years after Big Bang
D) ~100 million years after Big Bang

Answer: A
Explanation: z=14.2 corresponds to ~290 Myr post-Big Bang, near cosmic dawn.

## Quiz 2

**Stoics say: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' Which cognitive bias does this mirror? 🧠**

A) Negativity bias
B) Impact bias
C) Confirmation bias
D) Availability heuristic

Answer: B
Explanation: Impact bias: we overestimate emotional intensity/duration of future events.
